Send files between your iPhone, iPad, Mac, Windows PC and Android devices: wireless, fast and private.

Mac to Windows · Win to Mac · iOS to Android · Android to iOS · All Devices All Transfer

NO CLOUD
Your files are never uploaded to the internet or to any server. They travel directly between devices on the same Wi-Fi network.

SECURITY CODE
For every transfer a 6-digit code appears on the receiver's screen. Nothing is sent until the sender types it. You can skip the code for devices you trust.

END-TO-END ENCRYPTED
Data transfers between AllDrop apps are end-to-end encrypted with mutually authenticated TLS. Each device's identity comes from its own key, so no other device can read or impersonate it.

WORKS WITH EVERY DEVICE
• Free AllDrop apps for Windows 7, 8, 10 and 11, macOS, Linux and Android
• Devices without the app join from the browser by scanning a QR code
• Any number of files and folders at once: send a whole folder with its sub folders in one go
• Photos and videos in original quality, many at a time, no size limit
• Send to AllDrop from other apps with Share
• Received files are in the "AllDrop" folder of the Files app

No account, no sign-up, no ads. No data is collected.

Note: keep AllDrop open while receiving, and keep both devices on the same Wi-Fi network.
