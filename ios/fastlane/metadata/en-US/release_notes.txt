Version 1.1
• Send many files and folders at once: add any number of files, folders, photos and videos to the "To send" list, tap a device and they all go together.
• Folders keep their sub folders and arrive with the same structure.
• Photos and videos are picked in original quality.
• Devices connected from a browser can download each incoming batch as one ZIP.
• Choose where received files are saved: Settings → Folder for received files (computers and iPhone/iPad).
• "Open folder" and "Open file" now work reliably on Windows.
